## What was run
`react/public/config/Manual_TempSweep_53min.json`, generated by `py/tools/make_manual_temp_sweep.py`.
53 min, 11:40:27-12:33:32 local. 2 min lead-in at 0 rpm / SV 40, then five levels (SV 40, 55, 70, 85, 100 C)
of 10 min, each one staircase 600-1200-1800-2400-3000-2400-1800-1200-600-0 rpm at 1 min/step, then 1 min
at rest with SV 25. Result: 264 sweeps, 0 skipped, 11 OE captures, full schedule completed.

Each sweep: UL / AE / SP, **500,000 points per channel at 5 MS/s over a 0.1 s window** (raw samples, not
screenshots), with `telem_*` and `tick`. Scope FFT was off on the screen, so no `/FFT` group.

## How it differs from the 13 h / 4h40 runs
- **The VFD was operated by hand and RS485 was unplugged from it** (to take that noise source off the bus).
  The Omron (heater set-point, PV) and the tacho ran as normal. The runner printed the Hz to set
  (`control.manual_drive: true`, see `py/test_runner.py`): 10.08 / 20.17 / 30.25 / 40.34 / 50.00 Hz.
  50.00 not 50.42, because the drive's own limit (00-12) is 50.00 Hz -> ~2963 rpm on the tach.
- **Scope settings were whatever stood on the scope** (UL 0.4 V, AE 4.0 V, SP 16 V, 0.1 s, 500 k points):
  the profile carries no `scope_channels`, `scope_fft` or `acq_points`. `acq_type NORM` and
  `trigger_sweep AUTO` still come from `config.json`.
- Heater switched on by hand before the run; the guard switched nothing off at the end (it had not fired) -
  heater switched off by hand, PV fell 95 -> 91 -> 88 C.

## Read this before analysing
- **`telem_vfd_cmd_hz` is the PLANNED frequency, not the one set.** The operator typically hit +-1 %, worst
  +4 % at 10 Hz (10.49 and 10.51 Hz). **`telem_rpm_meas` (tacho) is the speed of record.**
- **Step changes happen +-5-15 s from the plan**, by hand. Sweeps within ~15 s of a step lie in a transition;
  select on a stable `rpm_meas`, not on `telem_rpm_target` alone.
- The first 10 s of the 600 rpm step of level 1 are stamped 0 rpm in the target but the shaft was already
  turning (motor started early).
- PV sits 1-4 C above SV on the high-speed steps (friction heat), as in 4h40.
- **This is not the SNR run** requested on the bus (no matched motor-only reference). It does give a
  motor-off side for a 1 kHz check: the 0 rpm steps (lead-in + one per level).
- Lubricated before the run. Same hardware boundary as 2026-10-27 (UL/AE on the linear supply, Faraday cage).
